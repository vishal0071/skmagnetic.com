## How to choose a magnetizing machine

Whether you call it a magnetizer, a magnet charging machine or a magnetiser machine, the right choice depends on what you are magnetizing and how many parts you need per hour:

| Consideration | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Magnet material** | Ferrite needs a much lower magnetizing field than NdFeB or samarium cobalt, so the material sets the energy and field required. |
| **Part size and shape** | Larger magnets and assemblies need more stored energy and a larger coil or fixture. |
| **Pole pattern** | Axial, radial and multi-pole magnetizing each need a different fixture design. |
| **Production rate** | Cycle time depends on energy per pulse, charging power and fixture cooling. |
| **Quality checks** | Peak-current monitoring and flux or gauss testing help confirm every part is fully magnetized. |
