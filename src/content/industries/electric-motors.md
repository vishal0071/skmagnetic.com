## Magnetizing in motor production

Most permanent magnet motor manufacturers assemble their magnets unmagnetized and magnetize the finished rotor or housing as one of the last steps. Unmagnetized magnets do not snap together, chip or collect steel swarf, so they are easier to store, handle and feed into automated assembly. The part is then magnetized by a single pulse from a [capacitor discharge magnetizer](/products/magnetizing-machines/magnet-charging-machine/). The pulse typically lasts a few milliseconds.

## Motor types and what they need

### BLDC and PMSM motors

Inner-rotor BLDC and PMSM motors are magnetized in a fixture that surrounds the rotor. Outer-rotor designs use a fixture placed inside the rotor cup. A dedicated [rotor magnetizer](/products/magnetizing-machines/rotor-magnetizer/) pairs the machine and fixture for this job. Pole count, pole accuracy and skew are all set by the fixture design.

### PMDC motors

PMDC motors usually carry ferrite arc magnets glued or clipped into a steel housing. These are typically magnetized radially from the inside in two or four poles. See [motor housing and stator magnetizing](/applications/motor-housing-magnetizing/) for how this works.

### Servo and EV / e-bike motors

Servo and traction motors often use high-coercivity NdFeB grades chosen for temperature stability. Sintered NdFeB typically needs a field of roughly 2.5–3.5 T to saturate, compared with about 1–1.5 T for ferrite, and high-coercivity grades need more than standard grades. Larger rotors also need more stored energy, and fixtures strong enough to withstand the forces of each pulse.

## Quality control on the line

A magnetizing station can do more than magnetize. Peak-current monitoring confirms that each pulse reached its set level, and a flux or gauss check of the finished rotor confirms the result. Together they help keep torque and back-EMF consistent across a batch. Our article on [magnetizing field requirements](/blog/magnetizing-field-requirements/) explains how material and grade set the field you need.
