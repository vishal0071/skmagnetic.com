This policy applies to business (B2B) orders supplied by **SK Enterprises**. Order-specific terms in our quotation or invoice take precedence.

## When we accept returns

We will review a return or replacement request where:

- The product supplied does not match the specification confirmed in the order
- The product was damaged in transit and reported as described in our [shipping policy](/shipping-policy/)
- The product has a manufacturing defect reported within the warranty period stated in your order confirmation {{Confirm standard warranty period}}

Please contact us before sending anything back, with your invoice number, a description of the issue and photographs.

## Items that cannot be returned

- Machines, coils and fixtures built or configured to your specification {{confirm}}
- Products that have been used, modified, installed or damaged after delivery
- Products ordered by mistake, unless agreed in writing {{confirm}}

## Replacement or refund

After we inspect the returned product and confirm the issue, we will offer a replacement, repair or credit/refund as appropriate. Approved refunds are made to the original payment method. {{Confirm refund timeline and method.}}

## Return shipping

Where the return is due to our error or a defect, we will arrange or cover return freight as agreed. Other returns, where accepted, are at the customer's cost. {{Confirm}}

## Contact

To raise a return request, [contact our team](/contact/) with your invoice details.
