This Privacy Policy explains how **SK Enterprises**, which operates the website **skmagnetic.com** ("we", "us"), handles personal information. We have written it with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 in mind.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us**, for example when you submit an enquiry or quote form, call us, email us or message us on WhatsApp:

- Name, company name, mobile number and email address
- Your location (city / state)
- Product requirements, quantities and messages
- Files you upload, such as drawings or specifications

**Information collected automatically** when you use the website:

- Device, browser and approximate location information
- Pages visited, buttons clicked (for example "Call" or "WhatsApp") and how you arrived at the site
- Cookies or similar technologies used by analytics tools — see [Cookies and analytics](#cookies-and-analytics)

## How we use your information

- To respond to your enquiry and prepare quotations
- To contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email about your requirement
- To process orders, dispatch goods and issue invoices
- To maintain business records and meet legal and tax obligations
- To understand how the website is used and improve it

We do not sell your personal information.
